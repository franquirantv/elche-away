# Visual and UX direction

## Hierarchy

Lead with **Elche Away**, **“Donde tú vayas.”** and the visited-stadium map on `/`. Owner-approved UX-01/02/03 direction (2026-10-24): navigation is Mapa, Viajes, Estadios, Resumen; all four destinations operate within the map experience. The summary reuses existing attendance totals and coverage, not new statistics. Stadium panels list visits; a visit opens a trip panel with fixture facts and personal memories. A dedicated expanded statistics experience remains deferred.

All public interaction occurs within a full-viewport map, inspired by an exploration application: no navbar, page header, footer or content below it. Identity, archive actions, information and controls float over the map. Panels have shareable query URLs; legacy archive routes redirect to those panels. Closing reveals the map; browser back/forward restores the selected content. Panning/zooming does not add history entries or change the URL. See [MAP_EXPERIENCE](MAP_EXPERIENCE.md) for the navigation contract and acceptance checks.

Trip cards prioritize opponent, score or “Result unknown,” date/season, then venue. Details show fixture designation, competition, geography, notes and a linked stadium. Stadium cards emphasize place and visit count; details list every visit. Special matches get a quiet accent and text label; avoid badges/achievement systems. Photos are a later enhancement, never required for a balanced layout.

Owner-approved MAP-01 cartography (2026-10-24): use OpenFreeMap Liberty with OSM/OpenMapTiles data. Keep streets, buildings and stadium geometry visible as the user zooms in; preserve the marker selection and URL-driven panel behavior. The custom local political/terrain experiments are parked.

## Relationship to the physical map

Use Elche-inspired green and white, charcoal text and restrained warm cork-toned accents. Travel references, stadium pins and a subtle paper/cork texture can connect the archive with the physical object. Avoid literal cork decoration behind dense content, excessive animation and reliance on a club crest or photography that has not been supplied/cleared. Clearly identify it as a personal fan archive, not an official Elche CF product. Core branding and layout must work without the Elche CF crest or any other copyrighted asset. Optional club crests can support fixture/stadium content when supplied; missing crests use accessible text/initials, and decorative images do not repeat adjacent club-name announcements.

Use a readable system font initially, simple spacing, and a small set of reusable cards/forms/buttons. No oversized component library or design-token program. Keep the home URL stable for a future printed QR code.

## Mobile and accessibility

Design single-column trips first, generous touch targets, visible focus and sufficient contrast. Never communicate result or special status through color alone. Map is the main surface on `/`; `/map` preserves old entry links by redirecting to it. Initial bounds remain peninsular Spain. Details use a nonmodal side panel on desktop and a bottom panel on mobile, with explicit Ampliar/Reducir controls and independent vertical content scrolling. Keep map attribution and zoom controls outside the panel. Unlocated stadiums and trips without a known venue stay accessible from the lists; their panels explain missing locations. Preserve server-rendered panel content and links without JavaScript or tiles. The map accepts drag, wheel/touch zoom and explicit zoom controls. A reset button returns to the complete initial peninsula view and closes the panel. There is no page scrolling; only panel content scrolls. Respect reduced motion, keyboard navigation, Escape to close and focus on the new panel heading after navigation. Include islands/autonomous cities when data warrants; avoid forcing all points into an unreadable mainland view. Marker redesign remains a separate backlog item; VIS-01/02 styling is scoped below.

Unknown dates/results are explicit labels, not blank broken cards. Keep chronology semantics consistent with PRODUCT. Loading, empty, failed-query and unavailable-map states explain what happened without exposing internals.

## Admin

Start admin with locally stored Elche fixtures, season/competition filters and a clear “Asistí a este partido” action. Unattended rows show fixture facts only; marking attendance enables personal notes and special status, with photos later. Unmarking confirms removal of the personal record while keeping the fixture. Provide “Añadir partido” for missing history and a separate explicit import/review flow once supported. Show source conflicts, unknown mappings and locked local corrections for review rather than quietly replacing data.

Use functional mobile-friendly forms, searchable/selectable existing references when needed, clear required/optional fields and a public-visibility reminder for attendance notes. Keep factual annotations distinct from personal memories. Season details show Elche's division separately from the fixture competition: a cup match must not look like a league match. Present conventional home/away team selectors and aligned scores; derive the opponent rather than asking for it again. A convenience default can place Elche away, but the owner must be able to swap sides or mark designation unknown (clearing both teams/scores with a warning if entered). Never infer designation from the stadium. Let the owner leave historical fields unknown. Slugs are generated once and are not editable in MVP; club names and optional trusted crest references remain editable. Show server validation next to fields, retain entered values after failure, and make save/delete outcomes clear. Require deliberate confirmation for deletion; show referenced-trip conflicts rather than removing history automatically. The public archive remains attendance-only and does not become a full results portal. Public pages have no editing controls except a discreet admin link if useful.

## Review before coding

Name, tagline and Spanish public language are decided. Supplied visual assets and featuring a personally significant match on Home are optional refinements, not implementation blockers. The map-first navigation and responsive panel decisions are approved for UX-01/02/03; they do not require new artwork. VIS-01/02 is implemented as a bounded proposal for owner review, as described below.

## VIS-01/02 · Diario de exploración

Propuesta implementada para revisión: mapa Liberty sin cambios, identidad flotante de diario personal, papel marfil (#fffdf6), tinta verde (#193e30), verde Elche (#155d43) y acentos de recuerdo (#83532d). Georgia local en títulos y cifras del archivo; sistema sans serif en controles y texto. Sin fuentes descargadas, imágenes nuevas ni dependencias. Trama CSS tenue solo en el panel, nunca sobre la cartografía ni detrás de controles. Tarjetas de viaje como entradas guardadas: borde verde, separación punteada de fecha/temporada y resultado legible. Los estadios son lugares de la historia, con sus visitas conservadas; los partidos especiales mantienen etiqueta textual. Sin logros, puntos, lugares pendientes ni cambios en el admin.

Presupuesto de movimiento: entrada de panel de 220 ms con desplazamiento de 8 px y opacidad; feedback de controles/tarjetas de 140 ms; selección del marcador existente de 160 ms. Sin animaciones por tarjeta al cargar, bucles, fuentes externas, librerías de animación ni temporizadores que retrasen navegación. El panel se sustituye inmediatamente al cambiar de selección; una nueva navegación interrumpe la entrada anterior. No se anima altura, tamaño, sombra o filtro. El mapa conserva su centrado interrumpible y respeta movimiento reducido. Los enlaces de navegación, estadios y viajes muestran «Abriendo…» mientras Next resuelve la ruta; el marcador conserva el estado de carga textual. No se inventa un porcentaje de progreso.

Con movimiento reducido se eliminan entrada, transiciones y desplazamientos de feedback; selección, etiquetas, foco y carga textual permanecen. Sin JavaScript los enlaces y el contenido del panel siguen disponibles. La aceptación visual definitiva y la comprobación en un móvil físico modesto quedan para Preview.
